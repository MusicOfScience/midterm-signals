METHODOLOGY

Simple enough to audit, explicit enough to argue with

01 · House national leg

Eight post-1990 midterms calibrate Democratic House seats against the national D−R House vote margin. The current generic ballot is used as a noisy proxy for that margin.

02 · House map leg

Current categorical House ratings are translated to fixed predeclared priors: 99.5/90/70/50/30/10/0.5% D from Solid D through Solid R.

03 · House blend

65% national seat-vote leg + 35% current map leg. Generic-ballot uncertainty, historical residual error and map uncertainty are simulated rather than suppressed.

04 · Senate race model

Ratings set the prior. Public polling margins are converted through a deliberately broad five-point effective error distribution and blended on the log-odds scale with capped weight.

05 · Correlated election

A shared national latent shock links House and Senate and induces 0.20 correlation among Senate race errors. Local errors remain dominant.

06 · Guardrail

Qualitative news, LLM judgement and presidential approval cannot silently alter numerical probabilities. Any future coefficient or override must be source-controlled and documented.

House D seats = 0.65 × national seat-vote draw + 0.35 × map-prior draw Senate race p = logistic[(1-w)·logit(rating prior) + w·logit(poll evidence)]

This is model v0.1: an operational baseline, not the endpoint. Its weaknesses are published alongside its outputs.
