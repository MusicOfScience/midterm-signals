Read before interpreting

Observed D−R is the Democratic-minus-Republican margin calculated from the two parties’ recorded votes. Difference subtracts the pre-election model's predicted D−R margin from the observed margin, in percentage points. Where votes are incomplete, both numbers are provisional and should not be interpreted as final swings or forecast errors.

Third-party and independent contests may have no valid two-party comparison; those cells remain blank. Senate races are statewide; House races are congressional districts. The model's Senate forecasts concern only seats being contested, while control of the Senate also depends on holdover seats.

Observed results are a separate data boundary. This page never modifies past forecasts or re-estimates model parameters. Frozen election-eve forecast · Normalised results JSON · Methodology / source contract.
